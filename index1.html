The problem

Why most influencer marketing fails

Fake engagement

You pay a creator, they post, the numbers look great — but those views and likes were bought. No real customers, no real ROI. Just wasted budget.

Verified only

Every IBBI influencer connects their social accounts for verification. We automatically flag suspicious engagement rates, bought followers, and inauthentic growth patterns.

Wrong audience

The biggest influencer in your country isn't necessarily the right one for your brand. Reach without relevance is just noise — and expensive noise at that.

Niche matching

Filter by niche, platform, follower count, engagement rate, location, and campaign type. Find creators whose audience actually wants your product.

Pay upfront, get nothing

Traditional influencer deals require payment before delivery. Many creators ghost, deliver poor content, or simply don't drive the results they promised.

Affiliate campaigns

With IBBI's affiliate model, you pay only when an influencer delivers actual results — clicks, sign-ups, or purchases. Zero risk, maximum ROI.

Our Story

We're solving a
€2 billion problem

Small businesses can't afford enterprise influencer platforms. Influencers struggle to find trustworthy brand deals. IBBI was built to fix both.

Our mission

Make influencer marketing accessible to everyone

The influencer marketing industry is worth over €20 billion globally — yet most of its value is locked behind expensive enterprise tools accessible only to large corporations.

Small and medium businesses have great products but no marketing team. Micro and mid-size influencers have loyal audiences but no easy way to monetize them. IBBI bridges that gap.

We believe that a local fitness brand should be able to collaborate with a local fitness creator as easily as Nike works with Cristiano Ronaldo. That's what we're building.

"Like Tinder,

Our founding concept — smart matching between the right partners
The problem we solve

Two sides, one broken system

Both businesses and influencers have been underserved by existing solutions. We listened to both sides before building anything.

Business pain points

Authenticity distrust

How do you know an influencer's followers are real? Fake metrics waste thousands in budget with zero ROI.

High platform costs

Existing platforms start at €2,000/month — completely out of reach for most small businesses.

Poor matching

Finding an influencer who reaches your exact niche audience is time-consuming, expensive, and often inaccurate.

Influencer pain points

Getting ignored

Small and mid-size creators spend hours on cold outreach to businesses that never reply. Demotivating and time-consuming.

Payment risk

No secure payment system means creators often do the work and never get paid. No contract enforcement, no protection.

No analytics

Most creators have no idea which of their collaborations actually drive results. No data means no leverage for better deals.
